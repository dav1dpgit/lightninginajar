Five reasons this is a fresh start

  1. 01

    Pure Lightning, real bitcoin

    On-chain bitcoin and a Lightning channel you hold. No Spark, no Ark, no custodian you can’t audit.

  2. 02

    Anyone with a node can be your LSP

    LiJ is built on the open LIJOX standard: if you run a Lightning node, you can serve LiJ wallets and put your bitcoin to work supporting the network — full control, no gatekeepers, and providers compete for your business.

  3. 03

    Your bitcoin key is your Lightning key

    The same 12 words work in any ordinary bitcoin wallet. Done with LiJ? Take your key and walk away, any day.

  4. 04

    Always-on is the LSP’s job. Control is yours

    The standard splits the work: the LSP stays online; the wallet keeps the keys, the privacy and the decisions.

  5. 05

    Runs anywhere. Even offline

    A WebAssembly app written in Rust — no app store, no account, no gate. Open it in a browser, keep it on your home screen.

Merchant register mode

One tap turns the wallet into a customer-facing display for your counter: a QR to pay, the amount in sats and your currency, and a clear “paid” the instant it lands. Japanese and English.

Own your theme

Delegate payments
